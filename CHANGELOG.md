# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of **Bingsu: Onigiri for iOS**, a fork of Onigiri by PeaceMonk (GPL-3.0) tuned for Kioku (desktop Anki on iPhone and iPad). This is the former build `2.0.3-Bento-ios.31`.

- Onigiri pages are laid out as a phone app: a Decks, Home and Menu tab bar, safe-area handling, page transitions and touch-sized controls.
- Settings fit a phone: every page opens at the top, controls stack under their labels, desktop-only options are hidden, and Settings follow the active theme.
- A labelled Add / Browse / Stats / Sync row on the deck list, and a Menu tab grouped by task.
- Study screens: the card-actions sheet, a full-height profile sheet, themed toasts, a one-row note-editor toolbar, and wrapping deck names.
- Anki scheduling, FSRS, review history, sync and collection data are not changed by this add-on.

Notes: the add-on package id (`onigiri_ios`), settings keys and saved data are unchanged from earlier builds, so upgrading keeps your settings. New strings are English in every language for now.
