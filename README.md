<h1 align="center">
	Bingsu
</h1>

Bingsu is an Anki add-on that transforms the standard interface into a modern, highly customizable dashboard, built to feel like a native app on iPhone and iPad through [Kioku](https://github.com/krmanik/kioku). Make your study space your own with personalized themes and layouts, then add a little fun to your reviews with mini-games, Pokémon companions, levels, and hexagon islands to build.

Bingsu only changes how Anki looks and feels. Scheduling, FSRS, review history, sync and your collection are Anki's own and are never altered.

### Get started

1. Download [`Bingsu-iOS.ankiaddon`](https://github.com/Fookwire/Bingsu_for_IOS/raw/main/dist/Bingsu-iOS.ankiaddon).
2. In Kioku open **Tools → Add-ons → Install from file…** and pick it.
3. Fully restart Kioku.

Updating from an earlier build keeps your settings and game data.

### Based on Onigiri

Bingsu is a fork of [Onigiri](https://github.com/thepeacemonk/Onigiri) by PeaceMonk, adapted for iPhone and iPad. The original desktop add-on is on [AnkiWeb](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/1011095603) (code `1011095603`). Onigiri was built to make Anki more comfortable to look at and easier to come back to, and Bingsu carries that goal onto the phone. Please support the original author.

### Philosophy

- **Anki is for everyone.** People study differently. Everyone should be able to shape their study environment around their own needs, preferences, and routines.
- **Design can encourage consistency.** Clear layouts, readable typography, and thoughtful details can reduce friction and make it easier to return each day. Good design can also [support motivation](https://www.mdpi.com/2414-4088/2/1/6).
- **Calm by default, powerful when you need it.** The experience should feel simple from the start, with deeper customization available when you want it.
- **Personalization without losing familiarity.** Bingsu builds around the Anki workflow you already know, rather than asking you to relearn how to study.

### Why Bingsu?

Anki already does an excellent job of helping people remember. Bingsu focuses on the experience *around* those reviews: the interface you open, the atmosphere you create, and the small rewards that make studying more enjoyable. You don't need to change the way you learn. You can simply make the place where you learn feel more like yours.
